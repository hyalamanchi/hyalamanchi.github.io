I've spent the last few years building AI systems that turn messy, unstructured data into something useful — OCR and NLP pipelines, LLM-powered document workflows, and the cloud infrastructure that keeps them running. Along the way I've learned a lot that never fits neatly into a resume bullet.

This blog is where I'll write it down.

## What to expect

I'm planning to post roughly every two weeks, alternating between two kinds of writing:

- **Technical deep-dives** — how I approached a real problem, what worked, what didn't, and what I'd do differently.
- **Industry reflections** — where I think AI genuinely helps (especially in healthcare), and where we should be careful.

## Why write at all?

Writing forces clarity. If I can't explain why a pipeline hit 95% accuracy — or why it *didn't* — then I probably don't understand it as well as I think. Publishing keeps me honest, and hopefully some of it is useful to you too.

If any of this resonates, [reach out](index.html#contact) — I'd love to hear what you're building.
