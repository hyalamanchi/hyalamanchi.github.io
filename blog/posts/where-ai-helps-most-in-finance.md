If healthcare keeps us well, finance keeps us stable — and both shape daily life for individuals, families, and entire nations. A lot of my hands-on work has been in the financial side: tax relief, statements, and business reports drowning in unstructured paperwork.

Here's where I think AI genuinely earns its keep in finance.

## 1. Automating document-heavy workflows

Finance runs on documents — tax forms, invoices, statements, filings. Much of it still gets keyed in by hand. OCR + NLP pipelines that reliably extract structured fields turn days of manual processing into minutes, and free people for work that actually needs judgment.

## 2. Catching what humans miss

Anomaly and fraud detection is a natural fit: high volume, subtle patterns, and a real cost to missing them. Models that flag the unusual — a duplicate claim, an out-of-pattern transaction — let analysts spend their attention where it matters.

## 3. Decision support, not decision-making

The best financial AI *surfaces* evidence — a summarized case, a ranked risk, a reconciled record — and leaves the call to a person accountable for it. Especially when money and livelihoods are on the line.

## The guardrails matter even more here

Finance is regulated for good reason. If AI touches financial data, three things are non-negotiable:

- **Auditability** — every automated decision must be traceable and explainable.
- **Fairness** — a model that quietly disadvantages a group isn't just a bug, it's harm.
- **Privacy & security** — sensitive financial data demands the same rigor as health records.

> Healthcare and finance are two sides of the same goal: helping people live stable, healthy lives. I want to build AI that serves both — carefully.

That's the throughline for a lot of what I'll write here.
